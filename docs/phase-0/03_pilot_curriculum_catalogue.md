## 2. Activity Rubrics and Competency Criteria

### Activity ACT-ELE-101: Identification and Handling of Electrical Hand Tools & PPE
- **Objective:** Student safely identifies, inspects, and uses combination pliers, wire strippers, neon testers, and safety gloves.
- **Criteria List:**
  1. `CRIT-101-1`: PPE Inspection and Fitting *(Safety Critical - requires ≥ 4/5)* (Weight: 25%)
  2. `CRIT-101-2`: Tool Selection & Insulation Integrity Check (Weight: 25%)
  3. `CRIT-101-3`: Wire Stripping Precision without Nicking Core (Weight: 25%)
  4. `CRIT-101-4`: Workshop Cleanliness, Tool Return & Safety *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 25%)

### Activity ACT-ELE-102: Making Western Union and Married Joints in Copper Conductors
- **Objective:** Prepare and twist copper conductors into high-tensile strength joints, apply flux, solder, and insulate with tape.
- **Criteria List:**
  1. `CRIT-102-1`: Cable Stripping & Conductor Burnishing (Weight: 20%)
  2. `CRIT-102-2`: Mechanical Twist Pitch & Joint Tensile Strength (Weight: 30%)
  3. `CRIT-102-3`: Soldering Quality & No Cold Solder Voids *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 30%)
  4. `CRIT-102-4`: 50% Overlap Insulation Taping (Weight: 20%)

### Activity ACT-ELE-201: Staircase Wiring Circuit with Two-Way Switches
- **Objective:** Install and verify two-way lighting control circuit for multi-level stairwells.
- **Criteria List:**
  1. `CRIT-201-1`: Schematic Interpretation & Board Layout (Weight: 20%)
  2. `CRIT-201-2`: Phase Wire Connected to Switch Terminals *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 35%)
  3. `CRIT-201-3`: Megger Insulation & Pre-Energization Continuity *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 25%)
  4. `CRIT-201-4`: Dual-Station Lighting Control Verification (Weight: 20%)

### Activity ACT-ELE-202: Installation and Testing of MCB and RCCB
- **Objective:** Mount MCB/RCCB on DIN rail, verify earth leakage sensitivity, and measure trip time.
- **Criteria List:**
  1. `CRIT-202-1`: DIN Rail Mounting & Tight Terminal Torquing (Weight: 20%)
  2. `CRIT-202-2`: Line/Neutral Polarity & Earthing Busbar Connection *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 30%)
  3. `CRIT-202-3`: Residual Current Trip Test Verification *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 30%)
  4. `CRIT-202-4`: Internal Conduit Cable Dressing & Labeling (Weight: 20%)

### Activity ACT-ELE-301: Direct-On-Line (DOL) Starter Wiring for 3-Phase Induction Motor
- **Objective:** Wire and operate a 3-phase squirrel cage induction motor with DOL starter and overload relay.
- **Criteria List:**
  1. `CRIT-301-1`: Motor Lead Star Connection & Insulation Test *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 25%)
  2. `CRIT-301-2`: Control Circuit (Pushbuttons, Contactor Aux Contacts) (Weight: 25%)
  3. `CRIT-301-3`: Thermal Overload Relay Full-Load Current Calibration *(Safety Critical)* (Weight: 25%)
  4. `CRIT-301-4`: Direction of Rotation & Running Current Verification (Weight: 25%)
